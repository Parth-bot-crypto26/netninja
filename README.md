# 🛡️ NetNinja – Cyber Safe Kids World 🌐🧒🧠

**NetNinja** is an interactive and playful digital space designed to educate children (ages 5–15) about cyber safety in a fun, engaging, and meaningful way. With vibrant visuals, animated tales, and gamified learning, **NetNinja** empowers the next generation to be confident, aware, and safe while navigating the digital world.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🏠 Home Page
A welcoming interface featuring animations, facts, and navigation help from our mascot **Blue**.

### 📖 Cyber Tales
Role-based, interactive stories with choices that teach kids cyber safety lessons in a narrative format. Each story concludes with a moral and tips.

### 🧩 Games
Includes quizzes, logic puzzles, and cyber-awareness challenges. Features:
- Star-based scores
- Achievements
- Leaderboard rankings

### 🤖 AI Chatbot
A child-friendly chatbot that provides real-time responses to children’s questions about cyber safety in simple language.

### 🎣 Phishing Simulations
Safe, realistic simulations to help kids practice identifying phishing attempts and unsafe online behaviors.

### 💬 Social Practice Zone
Interactive simulations that mimic real-life online conversations to help kids practice digital communication skills.

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Parental Dashboard
Allows parents to:
- Track their child’s progress
- View quiz results
- See leaderboard status

### 👩‍💻 About Us
Meet the developers behind NetNinja and find ways to get in touch.
